about

Inside your terminal, or instead of it. Zellij runs in any terminal emulator, and comes with a complete, modern terminal of its own, with image support, a tray icon and desktop notifications. The Zellij terminal →

Cross platform. Runs natively on Linux, macOS and Windows, on your computer or on a server over SSH, in a browser, even on a phone, with the web client →

Free and open source. MIT licensed, no ads, no telemetry and no account. Not funded by venture capital, but sponsored by the community →

panes

Panes, tabs and sessions

Sessions for each project. Tabs for separate tasks. Panes side by side for things you watch together. Stacks when you have more panes than room. Floating panes for a quick look or a one-off command, pinned on top if you like.

The basics →
inbox

One inbox for everything that needs you

See at a glance which coding agents, builds and long-running commands are working, done, failed or waiting on you, across every session. Answer directly from the inbox or switch to jump right back in.

Zellij for coding agents
terminal

Zellij as your terminal

Zellij comes with its own modern terminal, complete with image support and desktop integration. Get a tray icon that shows when something needs you. Receive a desktop notification when a command finishes, fails or waits on you. Click the icon to see, open and manage your sessions, even with every window closed.

Use Zellij as your terminal →
