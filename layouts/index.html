Pick up any session, from anywhere

  • zellij web in a browser, even on a phone

settings

Easy setup, right in the app

Change keys, options and themes from the settings screen, for this session or for good. The theme editor previews your colors and flags any that are hard to read. Prefer files? Settings save to a plain config file, layouts capture a project's workspace, and plugins extend Zellij in any language that compiles to WebAssembly.
